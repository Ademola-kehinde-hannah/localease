ABOUT LOCALEASE

Connecting our community, one service at a time.

LocalEase is a community services directory that helps residents of Ibafo discover local artisans and essential services more easily.

OUR PURPOSE

Why LocalEase?

Finding a reliable artisan can take time, especially when information is scattered across different places.

LocalEase gives residents one simple place to browse categories, compare ratings and reviews, and find location information.
