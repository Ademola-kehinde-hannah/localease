How artisan verification works

Before an artisan receives a verified status, an authorised LocalEase administrator should review the relevant information and evidence.

🪪

Identity check

Confirm that the artisan's identity matches their registration information.

📞

Contact confirmation

Confirm that the artisan can be reached through their registered phone number.

🛠️

Skill assessment

Review relevant certificates, practical experience or evidence of previous work.

⭐

References

Where available, check references from previous customers with their permission.

Important: Registration alone does not mean an artisan is verified. Only checks that have actually been completed should be displayed as verified.

Client safety guidelines

Keep these precautions in mind before hiring an artisan.

01

Avoid isolated meetings

Do not arrange your first meeting in an isolated or unfamiliar private location. Where possible, meet in a public place.

02

Have someone with you

If an artisan visits your home, consider having a trusted person present. Arrange visits during daylight where possible.

03

Confirm the artisan's details

Check the artisan's name, phone number, profile information and verification status.

04

Agree on the cost first

Discuss the work, estimated cost, materials and payment arrangements before work begins.

05

Protect your personal information

Never share your banking PIN, passwords or one-time passwords with an artisan.

06

Trust your instincts

Stop the interaction if you feel unsafe. Leave the situation and seek help if necessary.

Important safety disclaimer

LocalEase is a platform designed to connect clients with service providers. A verification status indicates only the specific checks completed and stated on an artisan's profile.

Verification does not guarantee an artisan's character, conduct, workmanship or future behaviour. LocalEase cannot guarantee the safety of every interaction or service.

Clients are responsible for taking reasonable precautions before hiring an artisan. Do not meet in isolated places or put yourself in an unsafe situation.

Your safety comes first.
